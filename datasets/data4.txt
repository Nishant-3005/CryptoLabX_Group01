Note: These frequencies form the foundation of frequency analysis attacks.
A ciphertext that preserves letter frequency is vulnerable to this technique.
Polyalphabetic ciphers like Vigenere flatten these frequencies, making analysis harder.
The Kasiski test and Index of Coincidence are used to find the key length first.
